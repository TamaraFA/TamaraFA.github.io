Research

Statistical Methodology

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical statistics and statistical machine learning, with a particular focus on nonparametric inference and kernel-based hypothesis testing. I develop flexible statistical methods for complex data, with an emphasis on establishing rigorous theoretical guarantees for their validity, power, and asymptotic behavior. My current research interests include composite hypothesis testing, adaptive inference, survival analysis, and statistical learning for structured data.

See Publications for published papers and ongoing manuscripts.

Selected Applied Research Projects

Alongside my methodological research, I collaborate with interdisciplinary teams on statistical problems in healthcare, epidemiology, and biomedical sciences.

  • Population-Level Mortality among Clozapine-Treated Patients in Chile (2003–2024) 2026–present

    Statistical analysis of nationwide pharmacovigilance data to investigate mortality patterns associated with clozapine treatment.

    Statistical analysis conducted jointly with Valeria Leiva in collaboration with clinical researchers.

    First Place, SONEPSYN 2026, Salón presentation category (PSS209).

  • Joint Modeling of Longitudinal Hematological Outcomes in Clozapine-Treated Patients 2026–present

    Development of joint longitudinal and time-to-event models to characterize neutrophil count trajectories and the risk of neutropenia under different hematological monitoring intervals.

    Statistical modeling jointly developed with Christian Araya in collaboration with clinical researchers.

    Abstract submitted to SONEPSYN 2026.

  • Transcriptomic Analysis of Hantavirus Infection 2026–present

    Statistical analysis of RNA-seq data to characterize gene expression patterns associated with disease progression and clinical severity.

    Statistical analysis conducted jointly with Christian Araya, in collaboration with virology researchers within SENTINET.

  • Interactive Epidemiological Surveillance and Statistical Analytics Platform 2026–present

    Development of a pathogen-agnostic platform integrating statistical modeling, spatial epidemiological analysis, interactive visualization, and AI-assisted data exploration.

    Co-led with Nicolás Rivera (Universidad de Valparaíso), involving students from Universidad de Valparaíso and Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, within SENTINET.
