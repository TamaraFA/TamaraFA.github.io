Research

My research focuses on the development of statistical methodology and machine learning methods, with particular emphasis on kernel-based inference, survival analysis, and hypothesis testing. I am also interested in interdisciplinary applications of statistical methods, particularly in biostatistics and epidemiology.

Statistical Methodology

Kernel Methods and Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Development of kernel-based methods for goodness-of-fit, independence, and composite hypothesis testing, with a focus on statistical power, asymptotic theory, and calibration. Current research includes corrected wild bootstrap procedures and adaptive kernel selection.

Survival Analysis and Event-Time Data

Development of nonparametric statistical methods for censored and time-to-event data, including kernel-based tests, martingale methods, and inference under non-proportional hazards. Current work investigates tests of the proportional hazards assumption and multiple testing procedures in factorial designs.

Statistical Learning for Complex Data

Development of statistical learning methods for complex and structured observations, with particular interest in point processes, distributional representations, and kernel-based distances for clustering and unsupervised learning.

Selected Applied Research Projects

Population-Level Mortality among Clozapine-Treated Patients in Chile (2003–2024)

2026–present

Statistical analysis of nationwide pharmacovigilance data to investigate mortality patterns associated with clozapine treatment.

Statistical analysis conducted jointly with Valeria Leiva in collaboration with clinical researchers.

First Place, SONEPSYN 2026, Salón presentation category (PSS209).

Joint Modeling of Longitudinal Hematological Outcomes in Clozapine-Treated Patients

2026–present

Development of joint longitudinal and time-to-event models to characterize neutrophil count trajectories and the risk of neutropenia, accounting for different hematological monitoring intervals.

Statistical modeling jointly developed with Christian Araya in collaboration with clinical researchers.

Abstract submitted to SONEPSYN 2026.

Transcriptomic Analysis of Hantavirus Infection

2026–present

Statistical analysis of RNA-seq data to characterize gene expression patterns associated with disease progression and clinical severity.

Statistical analysis conducted jointly with Christian Araya, in collaboration with virology researchers within SENTINET.

Interactive Epidemiological Surveillance and Statistical Analytics Platform

2026–present

Development of a pathogen-agnostic platform integrating statistical modeling, spatial epidemiological analysis, interactive visualization, and AI-assisted data exploration.

Co-led with Nicolás Rivera (Universidad de Valparaíso), involving students from Universidad de Valparaíso and Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, within SENTINET.
